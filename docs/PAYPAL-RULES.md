# PayPal AI Hackathon: official rules, as they apply to us

Source: https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules (read 24 October 2026).
The rules can change ("subject to change at any time"); **re-read them
before submitting**. Where anything here differs from the rules page, the
rules page wins.

## Dates (Pacific time)

| What | When |
| --- | --- |
| Submission period | 1 Oct 2026, 09:15 to **12 Nov 2026, 12:00 PST** (20:00 UTC, 21:00 Lagos) |
| Judging | 1 Dec, 14:00 to 15 Dec 2026, 08:00 |
| Winners announced | around 21 Dec 2026, 14:00 |

Our target: submit both entries on **11 November**.

## Eligibility

- Individuals of legal age where they live, teams, and organizations.
- Excluded: Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, and any
  country under comprehensive OFAC sanctions. **Nigeria is not excluded.**
- Excluded: PayPal and Devpost staff, judges and their families.
- A project must not have had funding, a contract or a commercial license
  from PayPal or Devpost. Remit and RedLine had none.

## Every submission must have

- [ ] **A project** that integrates the PayPal developer platform (sandbox)
      and an AI tool, model or platform. "Any AI tool works, as long as
      PayPal integration is central to the project."
- [ ] **New or significantly updated:** an existing project must be
      significantly updated after 1 October, and the submission must
      **explain how**. PayPal and Devpost decide what counts as significant.
- [ ] **It installs and runs consistently,** and works as shown in the video
      and the text.
- [ ] **A text description** of features and functionality.
- [ ] **Tools:** name the AI models and services used, and explain how each
      is used.
- [ ] **A functional demo:** a hosted URL, *or* complete setup and run
      instructions in the repo. A mockup or non-working demo does not count.
- [ ] **A public repo** (GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket) with all source code,
      assets and instructions, and an **open-source license file detected in
      the repo's About section**.
- [ ] **Free, unrestricted access for judges until judging ends.** Include
      any test logins, API keys or sandbox credentials needed, or clear
      instructions for getting access, in the README or the description.
- [ ] **A demo video under 3 minutes** on YouTube, public, linked on the
      form. It must show the project working on its intended device, with no
      third-party trademarks or copyrighted music or material without
      permission.
- [ ] **English,** or English translations of the video, the text and the
      testing instructions.
- [ ] **Original work, owned by us.** Open-source components are allowed if
      their licenses are respected and our work builds on them. Third-party
      technical help is allowed if the result is our own work and our ideas.
- [ ] Any third-party SDK, API or data is used within its terms.

## More than one entry

- Allowed: "each Submission must be unique and substantially different from
  each of the Entrant's other Submissions." Remit (refund firewall for AI
  agents) and RedLine (terms-backed checkout) are different products.
- **Prize limit per project:** one grand prize plus one sponsor prize, *or*
  one honorable mention plus one sponsor prize. No limit per entrant was
  found.

## How judging works

1. **Stage one, pass or fail:** does the project fit the theme and
   reasonably use the required APIs and SDKs? PayPal must be visibly central
   in the repo, the text and the video.
2. **Stage two:** five equally weighted criteria:
   - Technological Implementation
   - Design: a complete product, not a proof of concept
   - Potential Impact
   - Innovation
   - Presentation: the video shows it working end to end

   Ties are broken by the criteria in that order, so Technological
   Implementation decides first.
3. Judging "may utilize expert panels, peer review, automated AI-driven
   analysis, or any combination". **The README and the description must be
   clear and complete on their own;** a machine may read them first.
4. Judges "are not required to test the Project and may choose to judge
   based solely on the text description, images, and video". **The video
   and the text must carry the whole story.**

## After the deadline

- **No changes to the submission after the period ends.** The Devpost
  portfolio page may still be updated.
- Keep the repo public, and keep the hosted demo and the sandbox
  credentials working, **until 15 December** at least.

## If we win

- Identity and **our role in creating the project** are verified, and a
  winner affidavit must be returned within 10 business days of receiving it.
- Residents outside the US may need a **W-8BEN** form. Taxes and transfer or
  exchange fees are the winner's.
- Prizes are paid within 60 days of receiving the completed forms.
- PayPal and Devpost may use the project and the entrant's name, likeness and
  country in publicity.
